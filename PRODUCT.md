# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
Employers and collaborators assessing Baran Bozkurt, frequently from a phone. They need to understand his background, inspect actual work, and reach his CV and contact details.

## Product Purpose
A distinctive personal portfolio for a physics student and web developer. Present the person and verified projects through an illustrated, animated experience that works first on mobile.

## Brand Personality
Curious, tactile, irreverent. Orange, hand-drawn imagery, dolphins, narwhals and physics are explicit personal preferences. Subtle dark humor must remain appropriate for prospective employers.

## Anti-references
Generic AI landing pages, copied identities, placeholder accomplishments, desktop-only Framer layouts. Jackie Zhang's illustrated notebook and spatial motion are references for craft, not assets to reuse.

## Design Principles
- Make mobile a deliberate composition.
- Give curiosity a physical form through original artwork and interactive physics.
- Place actual work, identity and contact ahead of spectacle.
- Motion supports a readable page; never delay access behind a loader.
- Preserve /ahmetayazbey/ and its dependencies byte for byte.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard navigation, visible focus, WCAG AA text contrast, reduced motion, 44px touch targets, semantic HTML, usable without JavaScript. Standard accessibility choices under the user's authorization to design and implement independently.
