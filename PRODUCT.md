# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective clients, collaborators, and hiring teams who need a quick but memorable understanding of Baran Bozkurt's capabilities, working style, and selected work. They arrive from a portfolio link and should be able to scan the essentials on a phone as easily as they can explore the full desktop story.

## Product Purpose

Present Baran's web-development and AI-supported product-development practice as considered, practical craft. Success means a visitor can understand his background, strengths, current education, relevant work, and how to get in touch without animation being required to interpret any of it.

## Brand Personality

Tactile, curious, and quietly capable. The site feels like a maker's real desk: a personal collection of printed fragments, annotations, and careful experiments rather than a startup template or an AI-generated moodboard.

## Anti-references

- Generic timeline/card-grid portfolios and oversized career metrics.
- Over-animated “wow” effects that hide weak information hierarchy.
- Polished-but-anonymous AI illustration, decorative gradients, and copy that could belong to any developer.

## Design Principles

1. Make the working process visible through real artefacts, not generic labels.
2. Let movement clarify sequence and depth; never use it as decoration alone.
3. Keep the crafted texture disciplined: one coherent printmaking vocabulary, generous negative space, and a small number of meaningful accents.
4. Put legibility and useful context ahead of visual novelty.
5. Treat mobile as an authored reading experience, not a compressed desktop stage.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for an accessible, high-contrast experience with semantic HTML, visible focus styles, responsive layouts, descriptive image alt text, and a complete static/reduced-motion fallback. No essential content is introduced only through scroll animation.
