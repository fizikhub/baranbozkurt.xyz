# Merakın çekim alanı

## Art direction
A physics student's illustrated orbital field notebook. Charcoal surround, a tangerine cover, natural-history engraving, pencil-like marginalia, a pale interior and a sage laboratory. The central metaphor connects physics and making digital things, with dolphin and narwhal orbiting together. Inspired by the spatial care of Jackie Zhang's notebook; no artwork, copy or Framer runtime reused.

The visitor is an employer opening a link on a phone in daylight, curious but short on time. High-contrast orange and ink, immediately visible content, direct work/CV/contact navigation serve that setting.

Voice: inked, inquisitive, dry-witted. Bricolage Grotesque supplies the irregular yet precise headline rhythm; Caveat is used only for personal annotations. Fonts self-hosted. No fictitious metrics, degrees, dates, or testimonials.

## Composition
Desktop: a two-part illustrated cover, then a paper-like personal page, staggered project specimens, a working double-pendulum experiment, and a generous orange invitation. Mobile: a fully recomposed stacked cover, readable project sequence, full-width experiment, no sideways scroll or scroll trapping.

## Tokens
Orange #f77732; ink #202320; paper #f2f2ec; lab #d7ddca. Dark orange text #bd4510 on light surfaces. Display 800, headings 600, body 400. Native links and buttons have visible keyboard focus. Small corner radii, no ornamental shadows.

## Motion
Quiet 9-second engraving drift, 38-second orbital satellite, responsive pointer tilt on precise pointers, a 46-second vocabulary ribbon, distinct paper arrivals, and a deterministic fixed-timestep RK4 double-pendulum simulation. CSS scroll-driven cover tilt progressively enhances supporting browsers. No scroll hijack or loading gate. Offscreen and hidden-tab simulation sleeps. Both system reduced motion and a persistent manual motion preference are supported.
